Overview

Expanded Public Works Programme

The Expanded Public Works Programme is a government initiative aimed at skilling and developing SA's ordinary men and women so that they achieve gainful employment and thereby alleviate poverty and joblessness.

At the time of the EPWP launch, South Africa's unemployment figures were hovering around 40%. The task of the agency was to bring forward a launch strategy and communications campaign that would reach all South Africans, without over-promising whilst resonating with the affected.

Our Task: Launch the EPWP, create awareness, and position it as the pre-eminent skills development and job creation initiative by the SA government.

Solution & Strategic Insights

We developed a composite and widely representative identity for the programme's credentials as a multi-sectoral and transversal initiative. The campaign was implemented and rolled out in phases linked to government (national and municipal infrastructure annual budget / expenditure) as well as already existing on-the-ground skills development and employment creating programs and projects.

The communications campaign focused on creating visibility for these planned and implemented initiatives, thus profiling the overall programme's efficacy and achievements (i.e. job opportunities creating potential).

Phase 1

Launched using an already successful and on-the-ground "gondalshu" Public Works programme in the Limpopo Province.

Phase 2

Localised communications by conducting a series of municipality and provincial development sponsored activations and implementations.
