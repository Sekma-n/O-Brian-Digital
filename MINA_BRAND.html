Case Study

MINA BRAND

A bold health awareness campaign that redefined visibility and engagement for Mina Brand.

The Challenge

Mina Brand needed to break through the noise in a competitive health awareness space. The goal was to create a memorable outdoor campaign that would not only capture attention but also drive engagement with the Coach Mpilo health initiative.

Our Solution

We developed a vibrant, high-impact outdoor advertising strategy using bold visuals and empowering messaging. The campaign combined traditional billboards with interactive elements, featuring the tagline "I Choose Health" and a clear call-to-action for Coach Mpilo support services.
