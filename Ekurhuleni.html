City of Ekurhuleni
Brand CI Manual

O'Brian Group partnered with the City of Ekurhuleni to develop a comprehensive Corporate Identity manual — defining visual standards, logo usage, typography, colour systems, and brand architecture. The new CI ensures consistency across all municipal communications and reinforces public trust.

From stakeholder workshops to final guidelines, we delivered a future-proof system with print/digital assets, empowering internal teams to execute the brand with precision.
