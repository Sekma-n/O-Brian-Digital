The Challenge

Increasing destination awareness in a competitive market

Tourism KZN needed to cut through the noise and reposition the region as a vibrant, culturally rich destination. Low brand recall among domestic and international travellers was limiting growth.

Our Solution

Multi‑channel storytelling

We developed a high‑impact billboard campaign, targeted digital ads, and a cinematic brand film – all unified under the "Zulu Kingdom" narrative. The result was a seamless brand experience across roadside, mobile, and TV.
