Case Study

Centre for Supplier Development

Empowering local businesses through strategic branding and incubation support.

The Challenge

The Ekurhuleni Centre for Supplier Development (CFSD) needed to increase visibility among small and medium enterprises. The existing brand lacked a cohesive identity, making it difficult to attract participants and partners for its incubation hub.

Our Solution

We developed a complete brand refresh, including a new visual identity, outdoor banners, and digital assets for the CFSD Incubation Hub. The campaign highlighted success stories and created a strong sense of community, resulting in a 60% increase in applications and stronger stakeholder engagement.
