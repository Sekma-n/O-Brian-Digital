CASE STUDY • ETHICAL IMPACT

Kimberley Process
Certification Scheme

Global initiative to eradicate conflict diamonds — Strategic storytelling, visual identity, and advocacy assets.

Illuminate transparency,
empower integrity.

The Kimberley Process (KP) is a monumental international coalition bringing together governments, industry, and civil society to stop the flow of conflict diamonds. O'Brian Group partnered with KP representatives to develop compelling visual communication tools, high-impact imagery, and narrative assets that reinforce the credibility and global reach of the certification scheme.

Through immersive photography, brand assets, and a cohesive digital presence, we amplified the message of ethical sourcing — humanizing the certification process while elevating institutional trust.

Outcome focus

Enhanced stakeholder engagement through authentic visual archives, refined digital collateral, and consistent brand narrative used across diplomatic summits, media, and industry forums.
