The Project

Turning fleet into brand ambassadors

A deep dive into how we transformed Sourceworx's commercial vehicles into a cohesive, high-impact brand experience.

Sourceworx approached us with a clear goal: make their fleet instantly recognisable and reflect the precision, reliability, and innovation of their brand. We designed a full-vehicle wrap system that works across multiple vehicle types while maintaining a unified visual language.

From the bold colour palette to the strategic placement of logos and messaging, every detail was crafted to maximise visibility on the road and at job sites. The result is a fleet that doesn't just transport — it communicates.

  • Full-vehicle vinyl wraps with anti-scratch protection
  • Consistent brand identity across 12 vehicles
  • High-contrast typography for maximum readability
  • Seamless integration of contact & service info
