Doing Advertising That Keeps Working

Insights on strategy, brand essence, and lasting impact — by Mxolisi Tyawa

by Mxolisi Tyawa —@ObrianSA • Strategy & Brand

Advertising should work and be seen to work. And the longer it works, the better. This speaks to the need for strategy, or at least an optimal blend of strategy and tactics. While individual executions need to be refreshed, what the brand stands for needs to stand the test of time.

Here are some insights that have guided us through the years:

What are we actually selling?

We focus not on product or service attributes but on the benefits that flow from those attributes. Don't sell the drill, but the hole it makes. The consumer has always had the power — the digital age just made it more obvious.

Benefit over feature

Not ‘what’ but ‘where’ is the brand essence?

While it is necessary to rigorously define the brand, its real meaning is at the scene of consumption — where the consumer interacts with it.

Context matters

Turn the brand or service into a potent sign

Your product does not mean anything by itself. We need to make it stand for something. The ultimate goal is to become an icon — a sign that nudges the consumer to think and act in a particular manner.

Become an icon

Find a unique language and the right tone

A great part of the language is visual. The only way to stand out from the clutter is by speaking clearly and uniquely. Where marketing science meets advertising art, manage the process carefully and patiently.

Visual & verbal clarity

Stay the course, evolve cautiously

It takes a lot of effort to ignore the temptation to experiment and go with the flow. Whatever tweaks need to be done must be done cautiously and over time.

Consistency & patience

Advertising that endures is a blend of timeless principles and timely execution. Strategy gives it backbone; creativity gives it wings. At O'Brian Group, we believe in building brands that work — and keep working.
