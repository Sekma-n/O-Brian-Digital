Built on vision,
driven by purpose.

O'BRIAN was incorporated as a public company in South Africa on March 7, 1997 — a joint venture between Brian Nielsson (Denmark) and Mxolisi Evan Tyawa (South Africa). From day one, our mission has been clear: carve meaningful participation for black people in non-traditional, first-world sectors of our economy.

Today, we are a specialist project management outfit offering brand strategy, creative development, and implementation via a network of preferred suppliers and partners.

An independent, sustainable, and profitable black-owned enterprise.

Our Journey

Three decades of growth, resilience, and impact.

1997 – 2003 The Founding Years

Born from vision

1997: O'BRIAN opens for business. Within months, secured our first long-term contract with Soul City Institute for Health and Development — a testament to our early credibility and mission-driven approach.

2004 – 2010 The Growth Years

Expansion & excellence

• Consolidated presence in the public sector & made significant inroads into the private sector.
• Opened the Durban office in July 2007.
• Expanded across the border with a partner office in Gaborone, Botswana (2009).
• Recorded our best financial performance ever — R43.7m for the year ending 2010.

2020 – 2025 The Lean Years

Resilience in adversity

The global credit crunch contracted the industry, wiping out years of growth. Private sector support for black-owned agencies pulled back, leading to staff retrenchments, downscaling, and restructuring.

Yet, we endured — rebuilding, reimagining, and preparing for the next chapter.
