Our Timeline

From a bold idea in 1997 to a trusted name in brand strategy — every chapter of our story is built on purpose and partnership.

1

The Founding Year

1997 – 2003
  • O'BRIAN opens for business.
  • Secured first long-term contract in September 1997 (Soul City Institute for Health and Development).
2

The Growth Years

2004 – 2010
  • Consolidated presence in the public sector and made significant inroads into the private sector.
  • Opened the Durban office in July 2007.
  • Expanded across the border, starting a partner office in Gaborone, Botswana in 2009.
  • Record best financial performance: R43.7m for the year ending 2010.
3

The Lean Years

2020 – 2025
  • The global credit crunch led to industry contraction, wiping out several years of growth.
  • Private sector pulled back from supporting black-owned agencies, leading to staff retrenchments, downscaling, and restructuring.
