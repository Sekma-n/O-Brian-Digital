The challenge

Unifying a nation's story

Brand South Africa needed a refreshed, cohesive identity toolkit that could inspire civic pride and consistent messaging across government, private sector, and citizens. Fragmented brand expressions were diluting the “Play Your Part” movement.

The solution

Strategic & creative cohesion

O’Brian Group developed a comprehensive identity system – from logo lockups and colour palettes to digital-first assets, print templates, and multilingual guides. The result: a toolkit that empowered stakeholders and amplified national pride.
